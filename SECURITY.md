# Security policy

Please report vulnerabilities in the KeyTap bridge or the KeyTap iOS app privately to
**support@keytap.me** with "SECURITY" in the subject. Include the version
(`age-plugin-yubikey-phone version`, or the app version under Settings ▸ About), steps to
reproduce and the impact you see. Please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.

We acknowledge reports within a few working days and keep you informed until a fix is released.
Fixes ship as a new release here and, for the app, as an App Store update.

Supported: the latest release of the bridge and the latest App Store version of the app.
